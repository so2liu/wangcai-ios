## 我们处理的数据

WorthSnap 是一款本地优先的个人资产记录工具。你输入的账户名称、余额、负债、备注和月度快照默认保存在你的设备上。开发者不运营用于接收这些资产数据的服务器，也无法查看你的账本内容。

如果你主动开启 iCloud 同步，账本会通过 Apple CloudKit 存储在你 Apple Account 对应的 iCloud 私有数据库中。该数据由 Apple 的服务处理，并受 Apple 相关条款和隐私政策约束。

## 我们不会做的事情

- 不连接银行、券商或支付账户。
- 不读取交易流水。
- 不出售资产信息。
- 不使用资产信息投放广告。
- 不进行跨 App 跟踪。

## 应用内购买

永久版购买由 Apple App Store 处理。开发者不会收到你的完整支付卡或银行账户信息。Apple 会向应用提供验证购买权益所需的交易信息。

## 导出和分享

当你主动导出 CSV 或 JSON 文件时，文件可能包含敏感财务数据。文件保存位置和分享对象由你选择，请妥善保管。

## 数据删除

你可以在应用中删除月度记录、归档账户，或删除 App 及其本地数据。家庭创建者可以通过 Apple 的共享管理界面管理受邀成员或停止共享；受邀成员被移除后，设备会保留最后一次成功同步的本地副本，并提示共享已不可用。

## 儿童

WorthSnap 不面向儿童设计，也不会主动收集儿童个人信息。

## 产品性质

WorthSnap 仅用于个人记录，不提供投资、税务、法律或财务建议，也不承诺任何收益。

# Privacy Policy

Effective date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

WorthSnap is a local-first personal net-worth record. Account names, balances, liabilities, notes, and monthly snapshots are stored on your device by default. The developer does not operate a server that receives this financial data and cannot view the contents of your ledger.

If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your ledger is stored through Apple CloudKit in the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account. This processing is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy policy.

WorthSnap does not connect to banks, brokers, or payment accounts; read transaction history; sell financial information; use financial information for advertising; or track you across apps.

Lifetime purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. The developer does not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details. Apple provides the transaction information needed to verify your entitlement.

CSV and JSON files that you choose to export may contain sensitive financial information. You control where these files are stored and with whom they are shared.

The family owner can manage invited members or stop sharing through Apple's sharing interface. If a participant loses access, WorthSnap keeps the last successfully synchronized local copy on that device and reports that the share is unavailable.

WorthSnap is a personal record-keeping tool and does not provide investment, tax, legal, or financial advice or promise financial returns.
