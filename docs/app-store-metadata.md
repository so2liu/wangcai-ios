# App Store 商品页文案草案

## English

**Name**: WorthSnap

**Subtitle**: Monthly Net Worth Tracker

**Promotional text**: See your complete financial picture in minutes a month—without connecting a bank or tracking every purchase.

**Description**:

WorthSnap is a private, low-pressure way to understand how your net worth changes over time.

Instead of tracking every transaction, review the current balance of each account once a month. WorthSnap turns those check-ins into a clear history of your assets, liabilities, and net worth.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Add bank accounts, cash, investments, retirement accounts, credit cards, mortgages, and other liabilities
• Confirm unchanged balances quickly each month
• Track total assets, total liabilities, and net worth
• Review monthly trends and account-type breakdowns
• Share one private household ledger through iCloud
• Assign account ownership and a monthly updater without gendered roles
• Export CSV reports and a complete JSON backup

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

WorthSnap does not connect to banks, brokers, or payment accounts. Your ledger is stored on your device by default. The developer cannot see your balances or account names.

FREE TO START

Use up to 5 accounts and create 2 monthly snapshots for free. JSON backup and restore remain available to everyone. Unlock unlimited accounts, complete history, CSV reports, and future premium updates with one lifetime purchase. No subscription.

WorthSnap is a personal record-keeping tool. It does not provide investment, tax, legal, or financial advice.

**Keywords draft**: net worth,assets,liabilities,finance tracker,wealth,monthly budget,portfolio

## 简体中文

**名称**：旺财 - WorthSnap

**副标题**：每月一次的净资产盘点

**宣传文本**：不用连接银行，也不用记录每笔消费。每月花几分钟，看清资产、负债和净资产变化。

**描述**：

旺财是一款低压力、重隐私的个人净资产记录工具。

你不需要记录每一笔流水，只需每月确认一次银行卡、基金、证券、信用卡等账户的当前余额。旺财会把每次盘点整理成清晰的资产、负债和净资产变化。

你可以：

• 记录银行卡、现金、基金、证券、公积金、信用卡、房贷等账户
• 对没有变化的余额快速确认
• 查看总资产、总负债和净资产
• 回顾月度趋势和账户类型构成
• 通过 iCloud 与家人共享一个私密家庭账本
• 区分本人、其他成员和共同账户，并指定每月由谁更新
• 导出 CSV 报告和完整 JSON 备份

隐私优先

旺财不会连接银行、券商或支付账户，不读取交易流水。账本默认保存在你的设备上，开发者无法查看你的账户名称和余额。

免费开始

免费版支持最多 5 个账户和 2 个月度记录，所有用户都能使用 JSON 备份与恢复。一次性购买永久版，即可解锁不限账户、完整历史、CSV 报告和后续高级功能。不是订阅。

旺财仅用于个人记录，不提供投资、税务、法律或财务建议。

**关键词草案**：净资产,资产盘点,负债,财富记录,家庭资产,月度记账,理财记录
